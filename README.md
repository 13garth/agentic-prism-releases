# Agentic Spark

**Plan it. Build it. Review it. Keep moving.**

A free desktop workspace for developers and people building with AI. Bring your project files, planning board, AI conversations and code review together, with a clear view of what is running, waiting or ready for your attention.

[Download status](https://thedevelopermarket.com/download) · [The Developer Market](https://thedevelopermarket.com/) · [The Extension Market](https://theextensionmarket.com/)

## One place for the development cycle

- **Plan:** turn an idea into project tasks and work through the next steps with AI.
- **Build:** work in the editor, reference files and chat with AI with or without a selected task.
- **Review:** inspect changes and recorded checks before deciding what to accept.
- **Keep track:** see project progress and work that needs attention. Open the Control Panel with **Ctrl+K** when you need configuration or system tools.

Agentic Spark runs locally on your computer. Connect your own Codex account for AI features; your provider's plan, usage limits and policies apply. The application is free, and Agentic Spark templates/scaffolds have no subscription fee. Template availability depends on the release; a complete template library is not yet published.

Beta packages may be **unsigned**. Automatic updates are **off**; updates use a manually downloaded installer. Each published release will identify its version, exact filename, SHA-256 and platform checks, including checks not performed. An unsigned beta may require approval on a work-managed computer.

Once a package is listed, choose the correct operating system and processor, compare the downloaded file's SHA-256 with its release notes, and follow that release's installation instructions. Open the app, connect your own accounts, then choose a project and begin with a small task.
